ABOUT HIS LIFE

A Life Dedicated to the Nation

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was one of India's most respected scientists and public figures. He was born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, and grew up with a strong interest in learning and science.

He played an important role in India's space and missile development programmes. His contribution to India's scientific and technological progress earned him the nickname "Missile Man of India."

Kalam later became the 11th President of India. During his presidency, he became especially popular among students because of his simple personality and his encouragement to dream and work towards a better future.

Beyond his scientific and public achievements, he was known for his humility, dedication and love for young people. His life continues to inspire students to pursue knowledge, discipline and meaningful goals.

1960

He began his professional career as a scientist and later worked with India's space programme.

1980

SLV-III Mission

He was closely associated with India's successful development of the SLV-III launch vehicle.

1997

Bharat Ratna

He received India's highest civilian honour, the Bharat Ratna.

2002

President of India

He became the 11th President of India and served from 2002 to 2007.
